A QUICK REFRESHER

Bid. Choose. Take.

Four players. Thirteen cards each. Five rounds. İhaleli Batak: win the auction to choose trump.

  1. Open at 5–13 tricks. Raise the highest bid or pass permanently. The last bidder wins; a bid of 13 wins immediately. If everyone passes, the first bidder takes a contract of 4.
  2. Choose trump. The auction winner selects any suit and leads the first trick. Play and bidding go clockwise.
  3. Follow the led suit. Beat the winning card if you can. With no led suit, play trump and overtrump if possible. With neither, discard anything.
  4. Trump stays closed until played off-suit, unless your hand contains only trump.
  5. Make the contract: score tricks taken. Miss it: lose the contract value. Other players need at least one trick; take none and lose the contract value. Highest total after five rounds wins.

House rules vary. Disconnected players keep their seats; their turn waits for them. Refresh to rejoin. Rooms and signed sessions expire when the server restarts.
